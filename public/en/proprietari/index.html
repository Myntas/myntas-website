The benefits

Why tokenize your property.

Tokenization divides the value of a property into digital shares. For the owner it means capital, flexibility and simpler management.

Capital without selling everything

Turn part of the property value into cash and keep the rest.

You decide how much to offer

Choose the share to tokenize, from a small part up to the whole property.

Many investors, one transaction

On AUREA your offering reaches many investors, including those buying small shares. No negotiating with a single buyer.

A recognised value

The token price starts from a valuation report, shared with investors.

Diversify your wealth

Use the capital you free up to invest elsewhere, without giving up the property.

Management included

Rent collection, distributions to investors and reporting are handled by Myntas.

Structure and documents ready

Legal structure, offering documents and compliance handled by our team with our legal advisers.

Transparency for everyone

Every transfer of ownership is recorded on the blockchain and reports are shared with all holders.

A clear process

Every phase has defined documents and timings, from assessment to first distribution.

FAQ

Before you start.

Do I have to sell my whole property?

No. You decide which part of the value to tokenize. The rest stays yours.

What does it cost?

A tokenization fee and transaction fees. They are set out in the proposal you receive after the assessment, before any commitment.

How long does it take?

It depends on the property and the documents available. After the assessment we give you the expected timing for each phase.

Who manages the tenants after tokenization?

Myntas, with specialist partners: rent collection, tenant relations, maintenance and reporting to investors.

Which properties do you assess?

Residential, commercial or mixed-use properties in Italy, already let or with documented income, with land registry and planning status in order.

What happens at the end of the investment?

At the planned date the property is sold and the proceeds are shared as set out in the offering document. The terms are agreed with you during structuring.
