How an offering works

The life of an offering.

Every property on AUREA follows the same path, from publication to exit.

  1. 01OpeningThe offering is published on AUREA with its property page and documents.
  2. 02RaisingInvestors buy tokens, at €100 each, up to the planned total.
  3. 03AllocationOnce the raise closes, tokens are allocated to portfolios.
  4. 04RentRent is distributed periodically, in proportion to the tokens held.
  5. 05ExitAt the end of the planned period the property is sold and the proceeds are shared among token holders.
