Cookie policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Provisional version of 18 October 2026. This text is under legal review and may be updated.

In short

myntas.com does not use profiling cookies, analytics cookies or third-party tracking tools. That is why we do not show a consent banner.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Similar technologies, such as the browser's local storage, work the same way.

What this site uses

  • No cookies set by Myntas. The pages do not save cookies or data in browser storage.
  • Resources hosted on our domain. Fonts, images and scripts are served by the site itself, with no requests to external services such as Google Fonts.
  • Technical security cookies. The infrastructure that hosts the site (Cloudflare) may set technical cookies that are strictly necessary to tell legitimate traffic from automated traffic (for example __cf_bm). They are not used to profile you and do not require consent.

If anything changes

If we ever add analytics or other non-technical cookies, we will turn them on only after your consent, through a banner, and update this page.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking technical security cookies may prevent access to the site.

Contact

For questions, email info@myntas.com. For how we process personal data, read the privacy notice.
