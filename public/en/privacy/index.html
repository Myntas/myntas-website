Privacy notice

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Provisional version of 18 October 2026. This text is under legal review and may be updated.

This notice explains how Myntas S.r.l. processes the personal data of visitors to myntas.com and of people who fill in the forms on the site, under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003. The Italian version prevails in case of differences.

1. Data controller

Myntas S.r.l., Viale Spartaco Lavagnini 13, 50129 Florence, Italy. Certified email myntas@pec.it, email info@myntas.com.

2. What data we process

  • Browsing data. IP address, date and time of the request, page visited, browser and device type. They are recorded automatically by the infrastructure that hosts the site, to run and protect it.
  • Tokenization request (Owners page). Full name, company, email, phone, type, city, approximate value and letting status of the property, share to tokenize, free description.
  • AUREA early access. Name, email and profile (investor, owner or both).
  • New-offering alerts and newsletter. Email address.
  • Emails you send us. The data in the message.

Together with form data we record the language, the page it was sent from and the date. Please do not enter special categories of data (for example health data) or other people's data in free-text fields.

3. Why we process it and on what basis

  • To answer your request and assess the property you propose, including pre-contractual steps. Legal basis: pre-contractual measures taken at your request (Art. 6.1.b GDPR).
  • To send you the early-access invitation, new-offering alerts and the newsletter. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6.1.a GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To run the site and protect it from abuse and attacks. Legal basis: the controller's legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).
  • To comply with legal obligations and, if needed, defend our rights. Legal basis: legal obligation and legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.c and 6.1.f GDPR).

Fields marked as required are needed to answer your request. Without them we cannot follow up.

4. How long we keep it

  • Tokenization requests and contacts: up to 24 months from the last contact, unless a contract is started.
  • Early access, alerts and newsletter: until you withdraw consent or unsubscribe.
  • Browsing data: for the time strictly needed to keep the service secure, according to the hosting provider's settings.

5. Who can process it

Data is processed by authorised Myntas staff and by providers acting as processors: the site's hosting and network provider (Cloudflare, Inc.) and the email and contact-management providers we use. It may be shared with our legal and tax advisers and, where the law requires, with authorities. We do not sell data and do not use it for profiling.

6. Transfers outside the European Union

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, in particular in the United States. In these cases the transfer relies on an adequacy decision (EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework) or on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

7. Your rights

You can ask at any time for access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interest (Arts. 15 to 22 GDPR). You can withdraw consent without affecting processing already carried out. To exercise your rights, email info@myntas.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (www.garanteprivacy.it).

8. Cookies

The site uses no profiling cookies and no analytics tools. Details are in the cookie policy.

9. Minors

The site and Myntas services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes

We may update this notice. The date of the last update is shown at the top.
