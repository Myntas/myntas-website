Real estate tokenization

Bricks and mortar, in digital shares.

Myntas is changing how people invest in property. We turn income-producing properties into digital tokens you can buy with small amounts. Owners and developers can free up liquidity more simply and reach new investors. Investors can own a share of a property and receive their part of the rent, with every transfer recorded on the blockchain.

The property

Behind every token, a real property.

Residential and commercial properties that generate income, selected through technical, legal and tax due diligence before every issuance.

The shares

The property, split into shares.

The value of the property is divided into tokens. Investors choose how many shares to buy and receive their share of the rent, in proportion.

Illustrative example €1,200,000 ÷ 12,000 tokens = €100 per token

The process

From property to token.

IAssessmentWe analyse the property, its leases and its land registry and planning documents.
IIStructuringWith our legal advisers we define the structure and the rights of the shares.
IIIIssuanceThe shares are issued as tokens and offered through authorised partners.
IVManagementWe distribute the rent, handle reporting and look after the property until exit.
